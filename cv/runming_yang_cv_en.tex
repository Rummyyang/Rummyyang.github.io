\begin{rSection}{ONGOING RESEARCH}
{\fontsize{10.5}{13.5}\selectfont\textbf{\href{https://rummyyang.github.io/research/opd/opd_deck.html\#1}{\textcolor{researchtitle}{On-policy Distillation: Thinking-Mode Transfer and Subsequent RL}}}\hspace{1.6em}HKU\hfill 2026.09 - 2026.10\par}
Qwen3 0.6B / 1.7B / 4B students and an 8B teacher, with comparisons of teacher prompting, generation budgets, and stopping behavior. Accuracy figures use MATH-500 avg@4.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Thinking-mode teachers overcome saturation in standard OPD gains.} \textbf{\href{https://rummyyang.github.io/research/opd/opd_deck.html\#7}{\textcolor{researchblue}{Gains from non-thinking teachers decline with student size}}}, reaching zero at 4B. With a thinking-mode teacher, 1.7B / 4B still gain 13.3 / 9.5 percentage points over their base models at a 16K budget.
\item \textbf{Behavioral evidence supports transfer of the student's own thinking mode.} At 32K, 1.7B / 4B reach 90.9\% / 95.2\%, \textbf{\href{https://rummyyang.github.io/research/opd/opd_deck.html\#2}{\textcolor{researchblue}{approaching their own thinking-mode performance}}}, with similar results from self-distillation at all three sizes. Per-question accuracy profiles align more closely with the student's thinking mode and correlate significantly less with the external teacher, supporting gains from accessing existing reasoning capabilities.
\item \textbf{Distinguished solving a problem from delivering an answer on time.} After truncation at 2K, allowing 32 additional tokens for a \textbf{\href{https://rummyyang.github.io/research/opd/opd_deck.html\#3}{\textcolor{researchblue}{forced answer}}} raises 1.7B accuracy from 17.8\% to 72.8\%, showing that OPD raises the non-thinking accuracy ceiling while also causing length inflation. Post-answer \textbf{\href{https://rummyyang.github.io/research/opd/opd_deck.html\#6}{\textcolor{researchblue}{p(EOS) and cross-continuation probes}}} show that the teacher still favors continuation after the student's answer, indicating that stopping behavior does not transfer alongside the thinking mode.
\item \textbf{Characterized the benefits of OPD+RL and length control.} Under the same downstream RL setup, 1.7B starting from OPD reaches 87.3\% at a 16K budget, versus 81.7\% for direct RL. Short-run RL shows that rollout length affects the \textbf{\href{https://rummyyang.github.io/research/opd/opd_deck.html\#5}{\textcolor{researchblue}{trade-off between length compression and accuracy ceilings}}}. Propose reward adjustment using an adaptive length threshold at fixed rollout length to reduce redundant generation while preserving the reasoning needed for difficult problems.
\end{itemize}
