\section*{Research Highlights}
\textbf{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12716}{Shadow-FT: transferring fine-tuning updates}}\hfill{\small\color{muted}Equal contribution}\par
Studies weight similarity between paired base and instruction-tuned models. Fine-tuning updates learned on the base model are transferred to its instruction-tuned counterpart.\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16989}{PTQTP: low-bit LLM inference}}\hfill{\small\color{muted}Equal contribution}\par
Decomposes weights into two ternary trit-planes for multiplication-free inference. Reports 4.63-fold higher end-to-end generation throughput than FP16 for LLaMA-2 7B on an RTX 3090 (batch size 1; 512 input and 512 generated tokens).\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04778}{LoCa: logit calibration for distillation}}\hfill{\small\color{muted}First author}\par
Calibrates teacher logits for misclassified samples to improve knowledge distillation with limited additional computation.\par\vspace{4pt}
